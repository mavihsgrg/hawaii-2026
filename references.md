## ⚠️ Where the docs disagree
1. **First island:** decided **Oʻahu** (Oct 18). Docs 2 and 9 (Kauaʻi) are kept for reference only.
2. **Driving:** decided **no car anywhere** (Oct 18). Docs 8 and 9 assume rental cars: historical.
3. **Island hop:** **Nov 3** (decided); doc 4's Nov 4 hop is outdated.
4. **Nights:** decided **8 nights** (NYC Nov 9). Still confirm the partner's PTO.
5. **Prices** are Oct 12 snapshots. Re-price before booking.

## Constraints pulled from the docs
- Hotels **under $500/night including taxes + mandatory fees**
- 🎂 Nov 6 stays relaxed: beach/pool, couples spa, sunset dinner, then drinks and dancing ([`birthday.md`](birthday.md))
- Lactose intolerance: mention it at every dinner booking; dairy-free if there's a cake
- One major outing per day, plus an afternoon break
- No car → rides are pre-arranged where it matters (PacWhale has **no hotel pickup** → prebooked dawn taxi); pickup tours must serve the actual hotel

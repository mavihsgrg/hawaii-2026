## ⚠️ Where the docs disagree (resolve in `overall_plan.md` → Decision board)
1. **First island:** decided **Oʻahu** (Oct 18). Docs 2 and 9 (Kauaʻi) are kept for reference only.
2. **Driving:** the newer docs prefer **no driving** (tours + transfers); docs 8 and 9 assume rental cars.
3. **Island hop:** Nov 3 (itineraries) vs Nov 4 (flight comparison).
4. **Nights:** decided **8 nights** (NYC Nov 9) on Oct 18. Still confirm the partner's PTO cap.
5. **Prices** are Oct 12 snapshots. Re-price before booking.

## Constraints pulled from the docs
- Hotels **under $500/night including taxes + mandatory fees**
- 🎂 Nov 6 stays relaxed: beach/pool, optional spa, sunset dinner (Hotel Wailea Restaurant is the lead; Ferraro's/Spago/Monkeypod/Mama's are alternatives)
- Lactose intolerance: mention it at every dinner booking; dairy-free if there's a cake
- One major outing per day, plus an afternoon break
- No driving → harbor/airport rides must be pre-arranged (Capt Andy's and PacWhale have **no hotel shuttle**); pickup tours must serve the actual hotel

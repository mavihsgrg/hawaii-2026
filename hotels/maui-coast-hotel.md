**Which room for two adults?**
- **Value:** Kai Ocean View King (388 sq ft + lanai). Enough for two, ocean view.
- **Space:** Aliʻi Suite (835 sq ft, two lanais). Worth it if you'll spend time in the room.

**View caveat:** One TripAdvisor reviewer booked an ocean-view room and got a partial view on the lowest floor. **Request a high floor and an ocean view in writing.**

# Maui hotel research (Nov 3 – 8, 2026)

> Research date 2026-10-18. Prices are single-night snapshots from aggregators (Expedia, Travelocity, Tripadvisor, Booking.com) on different dates. They are **not** November 2026 quotes. Use them to shortlist, then get a live quote for Nov 3–8.
> Trip needs: 2 adults, rental car, a central base for Molokini (Māʻalaea), Haleakalā, a Hāna day, and the Nov 6 birthday dinner at Hotel Wailea. Budget under $500/night all-in.

## 🗺️ Where to stay on Maui

| Area | Good for | Drive to Māʻalaea | Drive to Haleakalā summit | Drive to Hāna | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kīhei** (Kamaole beaches) | Value, central, condos with kitchens | 25–40 min | ~1h 45m | ~2h 30m+ | Beach across the road at most hotels |
| **Wailea** | Luxury, Nov 6 dinner nearby | 35–50 min | ~1h 15m | ~2h 45m | Most expensive area |
| **Kaʻanapali / Kapalua** (West Maui) | Beachfront resorts, quiet, Napili | 45–60 min | ~2 hrs | ~3 hrs | Longest to Hāna and Haleakalā |
| **Pāʻia / Haiku** | Surf-town feel, closest to the Hāna road | 15–20 min | ~1 hr | ~1h 30m to Hāna | Few beachfront hotels, fewer amenities |
| **Hāna** | Stay on the road to Hāna | 1h 30m+ | ~2h 30m | ~30 min | Remote, few options |

Drive times are estimates from the island guide in [`../maui.md`](../maui.md).

## 💰 Budget options (under $400)
- Lahaina historic inns: budget, but the beach is a 5-minute drive away (Oyster).
- Kīhei 3-star hotels on South Kīhei Road: roughly $230–280 in April 2026 price data.
- Most "under $200" lists are for the cheaper end of Kīhei, not beachfront.

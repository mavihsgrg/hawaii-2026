## 🛏️ Room types

Sizes and beds come from the hotel site and third-party listings, which don't always agree. Nightly prices are not published per room, so check each row on the live booking page.

| Room | Size | View | Beds / sleeps | Notes | Live price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Club 1 Bedroom Oceanfront Suite** | ~1,011 sq ft (third party) | Oceanfront | 1 bedroom, king; sleeps 4 | Best beach view; biggest room. Likely the most expensive | Check live |
| **Club Diamond Head Oceanfront** | Not confirmed | Oceanfront + Diamond Head | Not confirmed | Diamond Head angle from the lanai | Check live |
| **Club 1 Bedroom Ocean View Suite** | 758 sq ft | Ocean view | 1 king + sofa bed; sleeps 4 | Good space for 2 adults, no sofa bed needed | Check live |
| **Club Partial Ocean View** (accessible) | 355 sq ft | Partial ocean from private balcony | 1 king + full sofa bed | Smallest ocean-view option; a good budget choice | Check live |
| Club Suite, 1 Bedroom, Resort View | ~589 sq ft (third party) | Resort / inland | 1 king | Cheapest suite option; no ocean view | Check live |

**Which room for two adults?**
- **Budget:** Partial ocean view (355 sq ft). Smallest, but it has a balcony and an ocean glimpse.
- **Best value:** 1 bedroom ocean view suite (758 sq ft). More space than you need for two.
- **Splurge:** Oceanfront suite. Only if the view is the reason for the trip.

**View caveat:** Partial and resort-view rooms face inland or onto the grounds. Ocean Tower ocean-view rooms can have Diamond Head angles. If you want the beach, ask for an oceanfront or ocean-view room in writing.

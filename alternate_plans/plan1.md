| 💵 Rough total for two | ≈ $8,400–10,650 | **≈ $7,350–9,200** with a condo · ≈ $8,400–10,250 at Maui Coast |

**Recommendation: 🟡 go Maui-only**, unless Pearl Harbor or Waikīkī is a must for either of you.
- **You were right about the flights.** NYC ↔ Maui is **$570 per person**. The Oʻahu routing is about $880 per person once you add the island hop. That saves about **$310 per person, ~$620 for two**, and up to ~$1,060 if you'd have taken the nonstop to Honolulu.
- **You were also right about the stay.** 8 nights at the same Maui Coast Hotel is **~$285/night**, against ~$339/night for 5 nights. Kīhei condos with kitchens are ~$215–230/night for the week.
- **The anniversary and birthday get better.** In the current plan the anniversary is the long Hāna tour. Here, Hāna moves to Nov 4, so Nov 5 and Nov 6 are relaxed days with a special dinner each.
- **Less hassle:** no separate-ticket island hop (the riskiest part of the current plan), and one check-in for the whole trip.

## 🏨 Where to stay for 8 nights (Oct 31 → Nov 8)

Base stays in **Kīhei** (South Maui), for the same reasons as now: tours pick up there, it's 13 min to Wailea for the birthday, and it's close to Māʻalaea for Molokini.

| **Kohea Kai Maui** (king suite with kitchen) | Beachfront boutique hotel, adults-oriented | **$1,817** (was $2,102) | $227 | Free cancellation. Rated 7.8. See [`hotels/kohea-kai.md`](../hotels/kohea-kai.md) |

- **A kitchen helps on a week-long stay.** Breakfasts and snacks for the Hāna and Haleakalā days save ~$30–50 a day.

🟡 **Anniversary splurge (optional):** one night at a Wailea resort. For Nov 5–7, Booking showed the **Andaz ~$1,060/night**, **Fairmont Kea Lani ~$1,080/night** and **Four Seasons ~$1,300/night**. That's well over your $500/night rule. The splurge here is the Nov 5 dinner instead.

### 💍 Thu Nov 5 · Anniversary

> A slow, romantic day up the mountain and along the North Shore, ending at Maui's most famous restaurant.

| 🌅 Morning | ☀️ Afternoon | 🌙 Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 😴 Sleep in after Hāna · ☕ slow breakfast on the lanai · 🚗 up to **Kula** (~35 min) | 💜 **Aliʻi Kula Lavender** farm (walk, lavender scones, ocean views from 4,000 ft) · 🤠 **Makawao** galleries and Komoda bakery · 🚶 **Pāʻia** town · 🐢 turtles hauled out at **Hoʻokipa** (late afternoon) | 🍽️ **Mama's Fish House** ~5:15 PM, sunset at ~5:48 over the cove · 🚗 ~35 min back to Kīhei |

- 🍽️ **Mama's Fish House:** "booked months in advance," so **check Nov 5 availability now**. Book only through their own site or **(808) 579-8488** (9 AM–9 PM HST); they cancel reservations bought on third-party sites. Card needed to hold it; $50 per person if you cancel within 24 h. Dress: resort casual. ~$300–450 for two.
- 🔁 **If Mama's is full:** check again 24–48 h before (when most cancellations happen). Backup: **Merriman's Kapalua** (clifftop sunset), or **Monkeypod Wailea** for something casual. Lahaina restaurants are closed.
- 🥛 Mention **lactose intolerance** when booking, and that it's **your anniversary**.
- 🚗 A **one-day car** makes this day easy (Kula → Makawao → Pāʻia → Mama's → home). Without a car: a private driver or rideshares, ~$150–250 for the day (rideshare is thin Upcountry; prebook the ride home from Mama's).
- 📸 Lavender farm and the Hoʻokipa lookout are great couple-photo spots.

## 🚗 Car or no car?

The current plan has **no car**, and this plan works without one too. But a week on Maui is where a car starts to pay off. Two days need one most:

| Day | No car | With a one-day rental |
|---|---|---|
| Tue Nov 3 · West Maui | Rideshare ~$150–200 round trip | ~$60–100 incl. gas; stop where you like |
| Thu Nov 5 · Anniversary | Driver/rideshares ~$150–250; thin rideshare Upcountry | ~$60–100; no waiting, door to door |
| Other days | Tours pick up at the hotel; Kīhei is walkable | Car would sit parked (~$21/night at Maui Coast) |

🟡 **Suggestion:** rent for **Nov 3 and Nov 5 only**, from a Kīhei/Wailea rental desk (Skyscanner shows Maui cars from ~$29/day). That's cheaper than both the rideshares and a full-week rental, and you never park overnight at the airport. Haleakalā (Nov 7) and Hāna (Nov 4) stay as guided tours, so nobody drives the hard roads.

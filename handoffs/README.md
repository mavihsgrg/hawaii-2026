# handoffs: context snapshots

Dated snapshots of what we know at a point in the trip: what we actually did, what's only booked, what was only discussed. Paste one into a new Claude / ChatGPT session to pick up where we left off, or save a new one here at the end of a planning session.

**Rules for every snapshot**
- Name it `YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>_handoff.md` and put the snapshot time (HST) at the top.
- Keep three levels apart: **✔️ done** (one of us said we were there), **✅ booked** (a confirmation exists), **💭 discussed** (an idea). Never upgrade one to another without a report.
- No Booking.com PINs, card numbers, personal phone numbers, email addresses, Gmail links or waiver/management links. Confirmation numbers are fine.
- When a snapshot changes the plan, also update [`itinerary.md`](../itinerary.md), [`bookings.md`](../bookings.md) and [`todos.md`](../todos.md). The snapshot is the evidence; those files are the live plan.

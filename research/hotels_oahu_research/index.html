Before you book

Things that could change the answer

The pool closure is at the other OUTRIGGERThe Oct 5 – Dec 18 pool and sun-deck closure is at OUTRIGGER Waikīkī Beach Resort, not the Reef. No Reef closure turned up. Its guests are being sent to the Reef's pool, so expect it to be busier. A quick call to the Reef settles it.
Halloween is your arrival nightKalākaua Avenue gets the state's biggest costume crowd on Sat Oct 31. Hotels facing Kalākaua (Moana, Hyatt Regency, ʻAlohilani, Marriott, Beachcomber) will be loud late. The Reef sits on Kalia Road and is calmer.
OUTRIGGER Waikīkī Beach is mid-renovationRecent reviews mention construction noise, dust and a closed lounge, fitness center and ABC store. It's also $807 a night for the cheapest king. Skip it.
Marriott balconiesBalconies were locked for repairs Jan – Jul 2026, and work was still going on in May. Get "balcony open" in writing before booking.
ʻAlohilani "Ocean View" is a stretchReviewers say "Ocean View" rooms don't face the water directly, and most standard rooms have no lanai. Only Oceanfront rooms look straight out, and those are over the cap.
Rates moveThe Reef's 13% "Late Escape Deal" can end at any time. Booking now still lets you cancel for free until Oct 28.

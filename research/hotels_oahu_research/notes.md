# Oʻahu hotel research notes (Oct 31 – Nov 3, 2026, 2 adults)

Researched 2026-10-18.

## How the prices work
- Live prices come from Booking.com (searched 2026-10-18 for 2 adults, Sat Oct 31 to Tue Nov 3).
- The 3-night total Booking.com shows already includes the resort fee, but not tax. You can check this by taking the total, dividing by 3 and subtracting the "per night" price: what's left is the fee. For the Reef that's $1,107 / 3 − $319 = $50, which matches the $50 fee. For Halepuna it comes to $0, and Halepuna has no resort fee.
- **All-in ≈ Booking total × 1.18712.** Hawaiʻi tax since Jan 1 2026 is 11% TAT + 3% Oʻahu county TAT + 4.712% GET = 18.712%, and it applies to the resort fee too. Sources: The Points Guy, Hawaii Magazine, Travel Market Report.
- Budget check: $500/night all-in × 3 = $1,500 all-in, so the Booking total has to be **≤ ~$1,263**.
- ResortFeeChecker lists fees with tax already included. For example, $59.36 = $50 × 1.18712. Its "last updated: December 2036" stamp is clearly wrong.
- Live rates change. Booking.com's "Late Escape Deal" (~13% off at the Reef, the Waikiki Beach Resort, the Beachcomber and Queen Kapiʻolani) may end at any time.

## OUTRIGGER closures — the "Oct 5 – Dec 18" hint is about OUTRIGGER *Waikiki Beach Resort*, not the Reef
- **OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort:**
  - Pool and Duke's sun deck are closed **Oct 5 – Dec 18, 2026**. Source: the resort's FAQ, as quoted by Debushofufu on 2026-10-07. Expedia lists the same dates (renovation Mar 2 – Dec 18 2026) and says they may change.
  - The Voyager 47 Club Lounge has been closed since Feb 28, 2026. The expanded lounge reopens Jan 2027.
  - The $50/day resort charge still lists pool access and is not reduced.
  - Guests are sent to the pools at the Reef, the Beachcomber and the Waikiki Paradise instead.
  - Lodging Magazine (2026-09-25) says the guestrooms are finished, but the public spaces, pool, retail and lounge are still under construction.
  - Reviews from Sep–Oct 2026 report noise, dust, draped elevators, a closed fitness center and a closed ABC store.
- **OUTRIGGER Reef:**
  - I found no closure of the pool or lounge at the Reef for Oct–Dec 2026.
  - Possible downside: the Reef is one of the pools guests from the Waikiki Beach Resort are sent to, so it is likely to be busier.
  - KSL (Outrigger's owner) has a long-term $100M plan to redevelop the Reef. I found nothing showing that construction is active now. The Reef's official FAQ blocked automated access (403), so call the resort to confirm.

## Events and construction, Oct 31 – Nov 3, 2026
- **Halloween falls on Saturday, Oct 31.** Kalākaua Avenue has the biggest costume crowd in the state.
  - In 2025, HPD put up barriers against jaywalking, lit some intersections and blocked a lane on the mauka side (Hawaii News Now, KHON2).
  - No official 2026 HPD closure plan has been posted yet. Check honolulupd.org road closures around Oct 25–30.
  - Expect crowds and noise from the evening until late, especially at hotels facing Kalākaua: Moana, Hyatt Regency, ʻAlohilani, Marriott, Beachcomber and the Waikiki Beach Resort. Ask for a high floor.
  - The Reef (on Kalia Rd), Halepuna, Prince and Kaimana are quieter on Halloween night.
- **Hawaii Food & Wine Festival:** the Oʻahu weekend is **Nov 5–8**, after their checkout. Events are at the Sheraton, ʻAlohilani, Hyatt Regency and Halekulani. It should not push up their rates.
- **Waikiki Beach Marriott:** balconies were locked for repairs Jan 5 – Jul 1 2026, and a May 2026 TripAdvisor reply said the work was still ongoing. Confirm the balconies are open before booking.
- **Moana Surfrider:** the renovation is phased and was due to finish by Mar 2026. I could not confirm the Diamond Wing is done.
- **Hilton Hawaiian Village:** the Rainbow Tower renovation finished in Jun 2026.
- I found no 2026 Kalākaua or Kalia Rd street-construction notices. The Kalia Rd repaving ran Mar–Nov 2025.

<a id="setup"></a>
## 🧰 Before you go: set up

- 📱 Install **Uber and Lyft** on both phones and add a card. Compare both for each ride; prices differ.
- 📇 Save two **Maui taxi** numbers (ask the hotel which ones they use). Maui rideshare is thin **before ~7 AM, late at night and on Sundays**, so early or late rides go to a taxi.
- 🧳 Pack so each of you can carry your bag up a curb: shuttles and taxis are fine, buses are not (bags must fit on your lap on TheBus/Maui Bus).
- 🏨 Ask **Maui Coast Hotel** about its courtesy shuttle: listings mention a free shuttle within ~2 mi (would reach Kamaʻole beaches and Kīhei bars, not Wailea or OGG). Confirm.

## 🌺 Oʻahu legs

### Sat Oct 31 · HNL airport → OUTRIGGER Reef, Waikīkī (9 mi, 20–45 min)

| Option | Cost for two | Time | Good | Not so good |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 🚕 **Uber / Lyft** ⭐ | $25–45 (up to ~$50 Friday rush) | 20–35 min | Door to door, fast, no booking | Surge at busy times; walk to the rideshare pickup zone |
| 🚖 **Taxi** (curbside) | ~$29 flat (Charley's Taxi) to $35–50 metered | 20–35 min | No app, waiting at the curb | Confirm flat rate before you get in |
| 🚐 **Shared shuttle** (SpeediShuttle / Roberts Hawaiʻi) | ~$30–50 ($15–25 per person) | 45–75 min | Fixed price, prebook | Several hotel stops |
| 🚙 **Private van / car** | $50–130 | 20–35 min | Meet-and-greet, nice after 11 hours | Pricey |
| 🚌 TheBus Route 20 | $6 ($3 each) | 45–60 min | Cheapest | ❌ Luggage must fit on your lap; not with suitcases |

**Pick:** rideshare or flat-rate taxi. Prebook a shuttle only if you want the price fixed.

### Sun Nov 1 · Hotel ↔ Diamond Head (3.4 mi, 10–15 min)

| Option | Cost for two | Good | Not so good |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🚕 **Uber / Lyft** ⭐ | ~$12–20 each way | Drops you at the crater entrance; rides are plentiful in Waikīkī | Book the ride back from the crater lot after the hike (signal is fine there) |
| 🚌 TheBus (routes along Diamond Head Rd, check the current route) | $6 each way | Cheap | Stop is outside the crater: add a walk through the tunnel |
| 🚶 Walk | Free | Doable | ~1 h each way, uphill, on top of the hike. Not recommended |

### Mon Nov 2 · Hotel → Pearl Harbor → (Bishop Museum) → Hotel (12 mi, 25–40 min each way)

| Option | Cost for two | Good | Not so good |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🚕 **Uber / Lyft** ⭐ | ~$30–45 each way (check) | Fastest; easy to add the Bishop Museum on the way back | H-1 traffic in the morning |
| 🚐 Pearl Harbor shuttle from Waikīkī hotels | ~$15–25 per person round trip (check) | Fixed times, cheap | Fixed schedule; can't add Bishop Museum |
| 🎟️ Tour package incl. transport | $$ | Includes the Arizona program if tickets are sold out | Most expensive, fixed pace |
| 🚌 TheBus (Route 20 / 42) | $6 each way | Cheap | ~1 h each way; **no bags allowed at Pearl Harbor** anyway, so this works if you don't mind the time |

**Pick:** rideshare there (be there an hour before the program), rideshare to Bishop Museum, rideshare back.

### Tue Nov 3 · Hotel → HNL (9 mi)

Same options as Oct 31. **Leave ~2.5 h before the island hop.** Rideshare or taxi; ask the hotel bell desk to call a cab if the apps are surging.

## 🌋 Maui legs

### Tue Nov 3 · OGG airport → Maui Coast Hotel, Kīhei (13 mi, 30–45 min)

| Option | Cost for two | Good | Not so good |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🚐 **Shared airport shuttle** (Roberts Hawaiʻi / SpeediShuttle) ⭐ | ~$40–60 (fares from ~$17–18 per person ✔️; Kīhei ~$30 per person, check) | Prebooked, fixed price, meets you at baggage claim | A few hotel stops on the way |
| 🚕 **Uber / Lyft** | ~$45–65 (check) | Fast, door to door | Fewer drivers than Honolulu; wait times vary |
| 🚖 Taxi (airport rank) | ~$60–80 (check) | Always there | Most expensive non-private option |
| 🚙 Private transfer | $90–150 | Comfort | Pricey |
| 🚌 Maui Bus | $4–8 | Cheap | ❌ Bags on your lap, transfers; not with luggage |

**Groceries without a car:** Kīhei has supermarkets near the hotel; walk or take a short ride after check-in, or use a grocery-delivery app. No need to stop in Kahului.

### Wed Nov 4 · Kīhei ↔ Māʻalaea Harbor (Molokini boat, 9 mi, 22–40 min)

PacWhale has **no hotel pickup**, and check-in is ~45 min before an early departure, so you leave Kīhei around 5:45–6:30 AM.

| Option | Cost for two | Good | Not so good |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🚖 **Prebooked taxi** there ⭐ | ~$27–35 each way (estimate) | Guaranteed at dawn | Book the day before; agree the fare |
| 🚕 Uber / Lyft | ~$25–40 each way (check) | Fine for the **ride back** at midday | ❌ Risky for the early run: few drivers before 7 AM |
| 🚌 Maui Bus Route 10 (Kīhei Islander) | $4 each way | Cheap | Hourly from ~5:30 AM (check); stop location at the harbor has changed before; too tight for check-in |
| 🔁 **Switch boats**: a Molokini trip with South Maui hotel pickup, or one leaving from the **Kīhei boat ramp** | varies | Removes the problem; Kīhei ramp is a 5–10 min ride | Different operator; compare reviews and price |

**Pick:** prebooked taxi out, rideshare back. If the taxi can't be booked, switch to a trip with pickup.

### Thu Nov 5 · Haleakalā sunset (guided tour) ✅

🚐 **Booked: Polynesian Adventure sunset tour, order GLUKMF. Pickup 1:40 PM at Maui Coast Hotel's street-side sign, back ~8:30 PM**, entry + dinner included. There's no realistic no-car alternative: rideshare can't wait at the summit and there's no signal for a ride back.

### Fri Nov 6 🎂 · Kīhei ↔ Hotel Wailea (4 mi, ~15 min)

| Option | Cost for two | Good | Not so good |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🚕 **Uber / Lyft** there ⭐ | ~$15–25 each way (check) | Easy at 5 PM | |
| 🚖 **Prebooked taxi** Wailea Village (Tikehau) → South Shore Tiki Lounge, Kīhei, ~9 PM ⭐ | ~$15–25 | Guaranteed at night | Book the day before; walk ~½ mi back to the hotel after |
| 🏨 Hotel courtesy shuttle | free? | If Maui Coast Hotel has one that reaches Wailea | Check |
| 🚌 Maui Bus Route 10 | $4 | Runs to Wailea | Last buses around 7:30 PM (check): no good after dinner |

Hotel Wailea sits on the hill above Wailea; Tikehau is a short walk or ride away in Wailea Village. Full evening plan: [`birthday.md`](birthday.md).

### Sat Nov 7 · Road to Hāna (guided tour, optional)

🚐 **Hotel pickup ~6:30–7:00 AM, included.** Confirm the tour picks up at Maui Coast Hotel (some use a nearby meeting point). Nothing else to arrange.

### Sun Nov 8 · Kīhei → OGG (13 mi, 30–45 min)

Same options as Nov 3. **Leave ~2.5 h before the flight** (2:08 / 2:31 PM departures → leave the hotel ~11:30 AM). Ask the hotel to hold bags after checkout if you want a last swim; shuttles need a pickup time booked a day ahead.

## ⚠️ Blockers and risks

| Risk | How bad | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Haleakalā tour cancelled for weather/closure on Nov 5 | 🟠 No car means no other way up | Booked (GLUKMF); recheck NPS conditions; Sat Nov 7 is the free backup day |
| **No ride at dawn for Molokini** | 🟠 You'd miss the boat | Prebooked taxi, or switch to a trip with pickup / from the Kīhei boat ramp |
| Thin rideshare on Maui at night and on Sunday | 🟡 Long waits | Taxi numbers saved; prebook the post-dinner ride on Nov 6 and the airport run on Nov 8 |
| Less freedom to swap days for weather | 🟡 Tours set the schedule | Ask each tour about weather rescheduling when booking |
| Hāna/Haleakalā pickup not at our hotel | 🟡 | Confirm the exact pickup point when booking |

## 💵 Cost: no car vs rental car (Maui, two people)

| | Rental car | No car |

| Gas | ~$40–60 | — |
| Hotel parking | check (if charged) | — |
| Airport transfers on Maui | — | ~$80–130 |

| Birthday dinner rides | — | ~$30–50 |

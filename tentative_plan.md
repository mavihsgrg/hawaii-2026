## Day by day

### Sat Oct 31: arrive Oʻahu
- ✈️ JFK → HNL nonstop, morning departure (Delta 10:00 AM → 2:58 PM or Alaska 8:55 AM → 2:10 PM): _to book_
- 🚐 HNL → Waikīkī: prebooked transfer
- 🏨 OUTRIGGER Reef (price first; fallbacks Prince Waikīkī, OUTRIGGER Waikīkī; < $500/night all-in)
- Evening: beach walk, early dinner near the hotel. It's Halloween; a party only if you still have energy.

### Sun Nov 1: Diamond Head + Waikīkī
- Sunrise/morning **Diamond Head** timed entry ($5/pp, gostateparks.hawaii.gov), rideshare there and back
- Brunch, then a relaxed beach afternoon (optional beginner surf lesson)

### Mon Nov 2: Pearl Harbor
- Morning **Pearl Harbor / USS Arizona** ($1/pp, recreation.gov; arrive an hour before the slot)
- Free afternoon (optional Bishop Museum). Pack for the island hop.
- North Shore tour dropped: November surf is for watching, not swimming.

### Tue Nov 3: → Maui
- Check out → HNL, ✈️ midday island hop to OGG (~$138 for two): _to book_; a separate ticket isn't protected, so leave buffer
- 🚗 Pick up the rental car at OGG → 🏨 Maui Coast Hotel, Kīhei (~$1,695 / 5 nts)
- Groceries, pool, casual dinner. No big outing.

### Wed Nov 4: snorkel
- **Molokini + Turtle Arches** (PacWhale, ~$440 for two) from Māʻalaea, ~25–40 min drive (no hotel pickup)
- Afternoon: lunch, pool/nap, dinner nearby. If cancelled: move to Nov 5 or Nov 7.

### Thu Nov 5: Road to Hāna
- **Guided Hāna tour with hotel pickup** (~$590–650 for two; check that it includes Waiʻānapanapa)
- If the week feels too busy, drop this first and make it a slow Wailea beach day.

### Fri Nov 6: 🎂 birthday
- No alarm, slow breakfast, beach/pool, optional couples spa
- Sunset → 🍽️ **Hotel Wailea Restaurant**, ~5:15–5:45 seating; mention the birthday + lactose intolerance
- No Hāna, no summit, no early check-ins

### Sat Nov 7: Haleakalā sunset
- Self-drive ($30 entry); leave ~3 hours before sunset (no reservation for sunset, capacity limits)
- Warm layers; the drive down is in the dark

### Sun Nov 8: depart
- Check out (ask about late checkout / luggage + shower), stay near base, return car at OGG
- ✈️ OGG → NYC, must land **Nov 9** morning (Alaska 2:08 PM via HNL or AA 2:31 PM via LAX): _to book_

## Key times
- Sunset on the Maui coast is about 5:45–5:50 PM HST in early November.
- NYC falls back Nov 1, so Hawaiʻi is 6 hrs behind NYC on Oct 31 and 5 hrs behind from Nov 1.

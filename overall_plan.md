## A: Balanced day by day

### Saturday October 31 — arrive on Kauaʻi

- Land at LIH, collect luggage and rental car, then check in. Allow roughly 60–90 minutes after landing for airport/car tasks, more if queues or delays occur.
- If staying in Kapaʻa, keep the evening near the hotel; if in Poʻipū, keep it on the South Shore. No cross-island outing.
- Beachfront walk, simple dinner, early sleep. If arrival is late, dinner/check-in is the whole plan.
- Carry-on: swimsuits, essential medication, one change of clothes, and chargers so the first evening survives delayed luggage.

### Sunday November 1 — signature Kauaʻi scenery

- Select **one**: a Nā Pali boat tour or a helicopter outing. Put it early in the stay so November 2 can potentially serve as a weather-rebooking day.
- Boat version: breakfast, drive to the confirmed departure point, operator check-in, several-hour cruise; lunch and a quiet afternoon afterward. Do not pair it with a North Shore road trip.
- Helicopter version: morning check-in and briefing, flight, lunch; a short local coast/town outing afterward. Confirm actual operating status, departure airport, duration, weight/baggage requirements, refund policy, and weather routing directly.
- Evening: relaxed dinner. A sunset is a bonus, not another appointment.
- If canceled: use the local beach/town day and try the outing November 2 if availability permits. November sea conditions can prevent reaching Nā Pali even when a boat departs.

### Monday November 2 — Hanalei and North Shore, or a local substitute

- Around 8:30–9:00: leave after breakfast. Use an earlier start from Poʻipū than from Kapaʻa.
- Late morning: Hanalei town, pier/shoreline, lunch and shops. Road/access status must permit the route; do not continue into restricted Wainiha/Hāʻena areas.
- Afternoon: a few scenic stops on the return, not every beach. Aim to return with time for a shower and dinner.
- Treat North Shore beaches as scenic walking stops unless lifeguards and conditions support water activity. The November surf season is part of the scenery.
- If rain/road restrictions intervene: stay around Kapaʻa/Wailua or Kōloa/Poʻipū as appropriate; use open shops, cafés, museum or hotel time. State-park waterfall trails are not automatic substitutes.
- If a garden tour appeals, book only an actual operating tour/date. The current Allerton calendar is not an every-day offering.

### Tuesday November 3 — Kauaʻi to Maui

- Breakfast and check out. No major sightseeing before a midday flight.
- Return Kauaʻi car, fly LIH→OGG on the selected itinerary, collect the Maui car, drive to Kīhei/Wailea and check in.
- Reserve a half-day for the whole transfer, more with connections. A short airborne leg does not mean a short transfer day.
- Grocery/snack stop, pool or short beach stroll, casual dinner. No summit or Hāna drive.

### Wednesday November 4 — snorkeling, then rest

- Early morning: guided Molokini or another suitable snorkeling tour. Confirm meeting harbor and check-in time; do not assume all tours leave from the same place.
- Late morning/early afternoon: return, lunch, shower and nap/pool time.
- Late afternoon: easy beach stroll and dinner near your base.
- Molokini is accessed by boat; tour destinations and snorkeling stops can change with conditions. Choose the experience/operator rather than treating one specific reef as guaranteed.
- If either of you is new to snorkeling, prefer instruction, flotation, easy entry and a guide over a fast raft/back-wall itinerary. A shore lesson is a valid substitute if an offshore trip does not appeal.
- Weather cancellation: use your November 5/7 flexibility to rebook rather than forcing a substitute ocean outing.

### Thursday November 5 — Hāna if desired, otherwise slow Maui

**Hāna version:** make this the only major activity of the day. Approximately 6:30–7:00 departure, breakfast/snacks ready; a handful of permitted stops, lunch and timed Waiʻānapanapa visit; return via the same main highway. Budget roughly 10–12 hours with stops from South Maui, possibly longer. Exact stop order follows the reservation slot and current road conditions.

Keep the priority list short: rainforest/coastal scenery, one food stop, black sand beach, Hāna area. Do not promise every waterfall, Pīpīwai, sunset, and a leisurely dinner. A guided tour allows both of you to watch the scenery instead of one spending the day concentrating on curves.

**Slow version:** breakfast, Wailea coastal walk, beach/pool, lunch, an optional short local outing, sunset. This is the first activity to drop if the itinerary feels too busy.

### Friday November 6 — birthday on Maui

- Slow breakfast and no alarm if possible.
- Morning: beach or pool, followed by lunch.
- Afternoon: downtime, optional prebooked spa treatment if you want it, plenty of time to get ready.
- Sunset stroll and a reserved birthday dinner. Restaurant and menu selection remains separate research; none is booked.
- Keep a rain backup with a pleasant indoor dining option. Tell the restaurant it is a birthday and discuss dietary needs directly.
- No Hāna, long summit drive, or early snorkeling check-in. This day is for enjoying the trip together.

### Saturday November 7 — Haleakalā summit

**Sunset version:** breakfast and optional short morning beach time. Early lunch; allow roughly 2.5–3 hours from South Maui to reach the summit area, plus queues. Target entry/summit access around 2:30–3:00 PM for sunset around 5:50–5:55 PM, following current NPS advice to arrive three hours early. In practice depart South Maui around late morning/noon, not after a long Upcountry lunch.

Spend the afternoon at viewpoints, with short walks rather than a major crater hike. Bring warm layers, wind protection, water, snacks and fuel. No food or fuel is available in the park. After sunset, drive back carefully; allow an easy late dinner rather than a fixed early restaurant booking.

**Daytime version:** summit viewpoints earlier, optional Upcountry stop on return, hotel by late afternoon. You trade sunset for an easier final evening.

**Weather version:** if summit conditions are unsuitable, use the morning to reassess and choose a South Maui beach day or an open Upcountry outing. Cloud-free sunset cannot be guaranteed.

### Sunday November 8 — depart Maui

- Check out, leave bags with the hotel if offered, and keep the day close to the base or airport.
- For a late flight: breakfast, gentle pool/beach time, shower/change if the hotel provides a hospitality facility, early dinner, airport.
- Return rental car and allow the airline’s recommended check-in/security buffer. Add driving, fuel stop, and rental return/shuttle time rather than treating it as a hotel-to-terminal taxi trip.
- Check the actual ticket arrives in NYC November 9. Hotel checkout date alone does not guarantee this.

## Driving allowances and where to stay

Hotel selection is separate, but location changes your driving. Kapaʻa is a more central Kauaʻi base for Hanalei; Poʻipū is closer to Port Allen and South Shore outings. Kīhei and Wailea are both practical for this Maui schedule. One base per island avoids packing and checkout overhead.

| Kauaʻi destination | From Poʻipū | From Kapaʻa |
|---|---:|---:|
| LIH airport | 30–40 min | 20–30 min |
| Port Allen boat departures | 25–35 min | 50–65 min |

## Timing and packing details that matter

- All daily times use Hawaiʻi Standard Time. Hawaiʻi does not change clocks; New York falls back November 1. Hawaiʻi is six hours behind NYC October 31 and five hours behind from November 1 onward. Your phone should adjust locally, but tour reservations remain Hawaiʻi time.
- Early-November Maui coast sunset is roughly 5:45–5:50 PM; summit guidance is roughly 5:50–5:55 PM. Use the actual day’s time before setting dinner, not a summer 7 PM assumption.
- Pack beachwear plus warm layers and a wind-resistant jacket for the summit; sturdy shoes for walks, refillable water bottles, sun protection, and a small day bag.
- No summit sunset followed by another sunrise summit/bike tour the next morning. Pick one experience.
- Early November humpbacks are a possible bonus, not a guaranteed main attraction.
- If landing LIH November 1 rather than October 31, shorten Kauaʻi to one full day and choose signature scenery OR North Shore. Do not compress both full-day plans into one day. Hotel dates must be corrected before purchase.
